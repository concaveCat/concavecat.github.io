Oscillation

Reason for oscillation is for any potential function $U$, in the neighbourhood of the equilibrium, the function can be approximated as a quadratic function.

$$ U(x)=U(x_{0})+\frac{dU}{dx}(x-x_{0})+\frac{1}{2} \frac{d^{2}U}{dx^{2}}(x-x_{0})^{2}+\dots $$

Based on which we arrive at the condition

$$ \begin{cases} U=\frac{1}{2}kx^{2} \ F=-kx \end{cases} $$

eg1. Buoyant on Water

A buoyant is floating on the surface of the water. Find the oscillation period if we press this buoyant down into the water by $\Delta x$.

Solution.

We find the expression of the force

$$ F=-\rho_{0}\cdot A\cdot\Delta x $$ and we arrive at the oscillation period.

eg2. Gas Tank

A gas tank of area $A$ has a lid of mass $m$. Find the oscillation period around the equilibrium.

Solution.

$$ PV=const. $$ Take the derivative,

$$ dP=-\frac{const.}{V^{2}}dV $$

Hence, calculate the force:

$$ F=dP\cdot A=- \frac{const.}{V^{2}}\cdot A\cdot Adx $$ $$ F=- \frac{const.}{L_{0}^{2}}dx $$

At equilibrium,

$$ PA=mg+P_{0}A $$

And it can be solved.

Different Cases of Oscillation

$$ \text{Oscillations}\begin{cases} \text{overdamped} \ \text{critical damped} \ \text{damped oscillation} \ \text{free oscillation} \ \text{driven oscillation} \end{cases} $$

The Math: solving ODEs.

General Model

We consider a horizontally placed spring connecting at one end to a wall and the other to a mass $m$. We now introduce friction on the ground.

$$ m \ddot{x}=-kx-b\dot{x} $$

This is a standard second-order ODE. We rearrange the equation:

$$ \ddot{x}+\gamma \dot{x}+\omega_{0}^{2}x=0. $$

where

$$ \begin{cases} \omega_{0}^{2}=\frac{k}{m} \ \gamma=\frac{b}{m} \end{cases} $$

$\omega_{0}$ is called natural frequency and $\gamma$ ( $\frac{\gamma}{2}$? ) is called the damping factor.

We consider the general second-order ODE in the following form.

$$ \frac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}}+P \frac{dx}{dt}+Q=0 $$

  • Natural Frequancy $\omega_{0}=\sqrt{ Q }$.
  • Damping Ratio $\xi=\frac{P}{2\sqrt{ Q }}$.
  • Damping Factor $\sigma=\frac{P}{2}$.
  • Damped Natural Frequency $\omega_{d}=\sqrt{ \omega_{0}^{2}-\sigma^{2} }$

We give the solution directly:

$$ x(t)=\begin{cases} Ae^{\lambda_{1}t}+Be^{\lambda_{2}t},,,,(\text{overdamped}) \ (At+B)e^{-\lambda t},,,,(\text{critical damped}) \ e^{-\sigma t}\cdot A\cos(\omega_{d},t+\varphi),,,,(\text{underdamped}) \end{cases} $$

where $\begin{cases}\lambda=-\xi,\omega_{0}\pm \omega_{0}\sqrt{ \xi^{2}-1 } \ \sigma=\xi ,\omega_{0} \ \omega_{d}=\sqrt{ \omega_{0}^{2}-\sigma^{2} },,,,(\text{if }\xi \text{ <1})\end{cases}$.
